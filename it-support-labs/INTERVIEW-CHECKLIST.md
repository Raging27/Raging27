# Interview preparation

Use these questions after completing the relevant exercises. They are prompts, not claims of experience.

1. How do DNS failure, TCP failure and an HTTP 500 differ?
2. Why can a site work in a browser but fail from a command-line client?
3. What evidence distinguishes a stopped service from an application error?
4. What does a healthy container process fail to prove?
5. How would you minimise the scope of a permissions change?
6. Why does a successful backup job not prove recoverability?
7. How do you verify a restored file?
8. Why can a duplicate SQL record be an observed symptom rather than the root cause?
9. What must happen before an account reset?
10. How would you prioritise an outage against a suspicious MFA report?
11. What belongs in an escalation ticket?
12. What information must be redacted from published logs?

For every answer, use: context → evidence → decision → verification → limitation.

## Honest CV wording

Before execution: "Building a practical IT-support lab portfolio covering networking, Linux permissions, Docker services and incident documentation."

After completing specific labs: name those labs and the tools actually used. Do not claim production support, customer incidents or tenant administration from simulations.
